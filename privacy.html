Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our Advanced Personal Finance Tracker application processes and protects your personal data in strict compliance with global privacy mandates, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Data Collection and Single Source of Truth

This application operates entirely as a client-side localized architecture. We do not maintain any backend cloud servers, nor do we collect, harvest, transmit, or share your financial data with any third-party entities. All transactions, values, and categorizations remain exclusively within your user agent sandbox (browser environment).

2. Cookie Authorization and Web Storage Utilization

To deliver a persistent user experience without compromising your tracking metrics across browser sessions, this app utilizes HTML5 Local Storage (localStorage) instead of traditional tracking HTTP cookies. The scope of cached identifiers is constrained to the following scope:

  • financeTrackerData: Stores raw data arrays representing your budget items (Title, Amount, Category, Date).
  • financeTrackerTheme: Stores your visual user interface state preference (Dark or Light mode).
  • cookiesAccepted: Stores your explicit consent configuration responding to our compliance banner.

3. User Rights under GDPR Mandates

Because your transaction tokens reside completely in your localized machine data layers, you retain unconditional mastery over your digital footprints:

  • Right to Rectification: You are fully empowered to modify or revise any financial item at any given instance using the built-in application forms.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You can instantly wipe clean all cached histories and storage entries by executing a system reset or clearing your web browser application caches.

4. Regulatory Consent

By engaging with this software ecosystem and acknowledging our explicit Cookie Authorization banner, you register formal compliance regarding these edge-case processing frameworks.
